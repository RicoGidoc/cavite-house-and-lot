📈 Philippine Real Estate Market Overview

Home prices in the Philippines continue to rise in 2026, although growth has slowed compared to previous years. Many buyers are now focusing on affordable house and lot developments outside Metro Manila, especially in Cavite, Laguna, Bulacan, and Rizal.

Due to infrastructure projects, expressways, and growing communities, provincial areas are becoming attractive locations for first-time buyers and investors.

✔ Buying earlier helps lock in lower property prices before future increases.

🏘 Why Many Buyers Choose Cavite

Cavite remains one of the top locations for affordable house and lot developments because of its accessibility to Metro Manila through CALAX, Cavitex, and major road projects.

Families and OFWs prefer Cavite because properties are more affordable compared to Metro Manila while still offering modern communities, schools, malls, hospitals, and future business developments.
