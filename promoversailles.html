About Versailles Alabang Village

Versailles Alabang Village is a high-end gated subdivision known for its French-Mediterranean inspired community, located across Ayala Alabang and near major business districts in the South.

It is developed as a luxury residential and commercial estate, combining upscale living, parks, lifestyle amenities, and excellent accessibility.

Location and Accessibility

Location and Accessibility

Location: Along Daang Hari Road near Alabang, on the Muntinlupa and Las Piñas border, directly across Ayala Alabang Village.

Subdivision Type: Luxury gated community with a European-inspired master plan featuring house and lot units, townhouses, and commercial areas.

Amenities: Clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, spa, sports courts, parks, playgrounds, and jogging paths.

Nearby Establishments: Schools, hospitals, shopping malls, and business hubs are accessible within a few minutes.

Accessibility: Convenient access to SLEX, MCX, Skyway, Cavite, Makati, and BGC.

Why Choose Versailles Alabang Village: Luxury living, secure community, excellent location, and strong long-term investment value.
