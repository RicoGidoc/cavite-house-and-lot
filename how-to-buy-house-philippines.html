🏡 How to Buy a House in the Philippines (2026 Guide)

Buying a house in the Philippines is a big investment. Whether you are a first-time buyer or an OFW, it is important to understand the legal process, financing options, and requirements before purchasing a property.

📌 Step-by-Step Process

1. Set Your Budget
Decide how much you can afford including down payment, monthly amortization, and extra fees.
2. Choose Location
Consider accessibility, safety, schools, hospitals, and future development.
3. Find a Property
Look for house & lot, condo, or foreclosed properties from developers or owners.
4. Check Documents
Verify the title (TCT/CCT), tax declaration, and ownership legitimacy.
5. Reserve the Property
Pay reservation fee (usually ₱20,000–₱100,000 depending on property).
6. Sign Contract to Sell
This outlines payment terms, schedule, and conditions.
7. Apply for Financing
Use bank loan, Pag-IBIG Fund, or developer financing.
8. Deed of Absolute Sale
Final document that transfers ownership to buyer.
9. Pay Taxes & Transfer Title
Pay CGT, transfer tax, and register with Registry of Deeds.
10. Turnover & Move In
After title transfer, property is officially yours.

📋 Basic Requirements

  • Valid ID (Passport / Driver’s License)
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  • Proof of income (Payslip / ITR / COE)
  • Bank statements (if applying for loan)
  • Marriage Certificate (if applicable)

💡 Tips Before Buying

  • Always verify the title with Registry of Deeds
  • Check for unpaid taxes or liens
  • Work with licensed real estate agents
  • Never rush—inspect the property first
  • Compare bank vs Pag-IBIG loan rates
